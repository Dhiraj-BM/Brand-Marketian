How to choose the best social media marketing agency in Delhi NCR

Followers do not pay bills. Here is how to separate a social media agency that grows your brand from one that grows a vanity metric.

BM Brand Marketian ·Sep 2026 ·7 min read

Key takeaways

  • Engagement rate and follower count are not business outcomes — ask what the agency reports beyond them.
  • A content calendar without a distribution and paid-boost plan is a hobby, not a growth channel.
  • Reels and short-form video need a repeatable production system, not one-off "viral" attempts.
  • Community management (DMs, comments) should be part of the retainer, not left to you.
  • Ask how organic social content feeds into paid ads and lead generation — silos waste content.

Delhi NCR has no shortage of agencies offering "social media management," most of them measured on followers and likes. Those numbers look good in a monthly report and mean very little to your revenue. Here is what to check when comparing social media agencies in the region.

Ask what gets reported besides engagement

Engagement rate is a useful diagnostic, not a business result. A strong social media partner also tracks profile visits converting to enquiries, link clicks to your site or WhatsApp, and — where relevant — which posts get repurposed into paid ad creative.

A calendar alone is not a strategy

Posting consistently is table stakes, not a strategy. Ask how content gets distributed beyond the organic feed — paid boosting of top performers, cross-posting to Reels and Stories, and how creative from social gets fed into performance campaigns.

Reels need a system, not a one-off shoot

A single "viral" reel is luck. A repeatable content engine — hooks tested weekly, a shoot day cadence, and edits that reuse footage across formats — is a system. Ask how many pieces of content come from each shoot and how often creative gets refreshed.

Who replies to your DMs and comments?

A meaningful share of social leads come through comments and DMs, especially on Instagram in India. Confirm whether community management — responding to enquiries, moderating comments — is included in the retainer or left entirely to your team.

Social should feed your other channels, not sit alone

The best-performing paid ad creative in India increasingly comes from organic social content that already proved itself. Ask whether the agency treats social as an isolated channel or actively feeds winning content into performance campaigns and email/WhatsApp follow-up.

A follower count has never once paid an invoice. Ask what converts instead.

Frequently asked

How much does social media management cost in Delhi NCR?

Plans typically start around ₹24,999/month for a single-platform managed service, scaling up with the number of platforms, posting frequency and whether video production is bundled in.

How many posts per week does a brand actually need?

Consistency matters more than volume — 4-5 well-produced posts a week that get distributed properly usually outperform daily low-effort posting. Ask any agency to justify their proposed frequency against your category, not a generic template.

Should social media and paid ads be run by the same team?

It helps significantly — when the same team runs both, winning organic content gets reused as ad creative immediately instead of sitting in two disconnected workflows.
