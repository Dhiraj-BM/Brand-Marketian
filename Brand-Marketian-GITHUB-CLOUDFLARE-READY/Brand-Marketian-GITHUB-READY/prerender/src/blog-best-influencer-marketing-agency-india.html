How to choose the best influencer marketing agency in India

Follower count is not a strategy. Here is how to pick an influencer agency that tracks performance per creator, not per post.

BM Brand Marketian ·Sep 2026 ·7 min read

Key takeaways

  • Follower count is the weakest signal — ask how creators are shortlisted on engagement quality and audience fit instead.
  • Performance should be tracked per creator, not just per post, so you know who to work with again.
  • The best-performing organic influencer content should be run as paid ads (whitelisting/boosting), not left to organic reach alone.
  • Contracts and usage rights need to be clear before content goes live, not negotiated after.
  • Ask for a real campaign report, not just screenshots of posted content.

Influencer marketing in India is a large, fast-moving and often poorly measured channel. Many agencies still pitch on follower count and "reach," neither of which reliably predicts sales. Here is how to evaluate an influencer marketing agency properly.

Follower count is the weakest filter

A creator with 200K followers and 0.5% engagement usually underperforms one with 20K followers and a genuinely engaged, relevant audience. Ask how the agency shortlists creators — engagement rate, audience demographics and past brand-fit performance matter far more than raw follower count.

Track performance per creator, not per post

A single good post does not tell you whether a creator is worth a repeat partnership. Ask whether the agency tracks cumulative performance per creator across campaigns — this is what lets you build a repeatable roster of creators who actually convert for your category.

Turn winning content into paid ads

The strongest influencer programs whitelist or boost the best-performing organic content as paid ads, extending reach well beyond the creator's organic audience. If an agency only plans organic posting and never mentions running influencer content as ads, you are leaving performance on the table.

Contracts and usage rights, settled upfront

Confirm before content goes live: usage rights (can you repost or run it as an ad?), exclusivity terms, and posting timelines. Negotiating these after a creator has already posted is where most influencer campaigns get messy.

A real report, not a screenshot folder

Ask what a campaign report actually looks like — reach, engagement, click-throughs, and where possible, attributed sales or leads, broken down per creator. A folder of screenshots with no numbers attached is not a report.

A creator with 20K genuinely engaged followers usually outperforms one with 200K and no real connection to their audience.

Frequently asked

How much does influencer marketing cost in India?

Costs vary hugely by creator tier — nano and micro-creators can range from a few thousand rupees to ₹25,000-50,000 per post, while larger creators command significantly more. Agency fees for managing the program are typically separate from creator fees.

Is influencer marketing better than paid ads?

They serve different purposes — influencer content builds trust and social proof, while paid ads scale reach and conversions. The strongest programs combine both, running winning influencer content as paid ad creative.

How many creators should a campaign use?

It depends on budget and goals — a mix of a few mid-tier creators and several nano/micro-creators often outperforms one large creator for the same total budget, due to more authentic engagement and lower cost per engagement.
