How to choose the best B2B marketing agency in Delhi NCR

B2B sales cycles are long and considered. Here is how to pick an agency built for that, not one repurposing D2C tactics.

BM Brand Marketian ·Sep 2026 ·7 min read

Key takeaways

  • B2B needs different channels than D2C — LinkedIn, email and search usually outperform Instagram-first strategies.
  • Content needs to support a long consideration cycle: case studies, comparison content, and sales enablement material.
  • Marketing-qualified vs sales-qualified leads should be a shared, agreed definition with your sales team.
  • Account-based approaches matter more as deal size grows — ask if the agency understands ABM, not just broad-reach ads.
  • Attribution over a long sales cycle is hard — ask how the agency handles multi-touch, multi-month attribution.

A lot of "marketing agencies" in Delhi NCR are built for D2C — fast, visual, impulse-driven. B2B is a different game: longer cycles, multiple decision-makers, and a sale that might close three to nine months after first contact. Here is what to check before hiring a B2B marketing partner.

B2B needs different channels

For most B2B categories, LinkedIn, targeted search ads and email outperform Instagram-first, visually-driven strategies. If an agency's pitch is heavy on Reels and light on LinkedIn or search strategy, they may be repurposing a D2C playbook that does not fit your buyer.

Content for a long consideration cycle

A B2B buyer researching for months needs case studies, comparison content, ROI calculators and sales enablement material — not just top-of-funnel blog posts. Ask how the agency plans content across the full length of your typical sales cycle, not just the first touch.

Agree MQL vs SQL definitions with your sales team

Marketing-qualified and sales-qualified leads mean very different things, and misalignment here is the single biggest source of friction between marketing and sales in B2B. A good agency will insist on defining this jointly with your sales team before campaigns launch, not after.

Account-based marketing, for larger deals

If your typical deal size is large and the buying committee is small and identifiable, broad-reach advertising is inefficient. Ask whether the agency understands account-based marketing — targeting specific named accounts — rather than only running wide-net campaigns built for consumer volume.

Attribution over months, not days

A B2B deal that closes five months after the first LinkedIn ad click is hard to attribute cleanly. Ask how the agency handles multi-touch attribution over a long cycle — an honest answer acknowledges the difficulty rather than claiming perfect last-click attribution.

A B2B buyer researching for five months does not need a viral reel. They need a case study that answers their exact objection.

Frequently asked

How long does B2B marketing take to show results?

Given typical B2B sales cycles, expect meaningful pipeline impact in 3-6 months and closed revenue attribution often taking 6-12 months, depending on your specific deal cycle length.

Is LinkedIn worth the spend for B2B in India?

For most B2B categories targeting decision-makers, yes — LinkedIn's targeting precision for job title, company size and industry usually justifies its higher cost per click compared to other platforms.

Should B2B companies bother with social media at all?

Yes, but differently than D2C — LinkedIn for thought leadership and targeting, rather than Instagram-first visual content, usually fits B2B audiences and buying behaviour better.
