How to choose the best SEO agency in Delhi NCR

Every SEO agency in Delhi NCR promises "page one." Here is what to check before you believe one of them.

BM Brand Marketian ·Sep 2026 ·8 min read

Key takeaways

  • Organic traffic and ranked keywords matter less than qualified leads or revenue from organic — ask for that number specifically.
  • Technical SEO audits should be the first deliverable, not an afterthought after months of "content."
  • For any brand with a physical presence, Google Business Profile work matters as much as blog content.
  • Ask how the agency measures visibility in AI-powered search (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews), not just classic blue links.
  • Link-building tactics should be disclosed in detail — vague answers usually mean risky, low-quality links.

Delhi NCR has one of the densest concentrations of SEO agencies in India, and almost all of them lead with "we'll get you to page one." Rankings for a single vanity keyword are the easiest thing to fake and the least useful thing to track. Here is what to actually check before signing an SEO retainer.

Ask for organic revenue, not organic rankings

Ranking #1 for a keyword nobody searches, or one that does not convert, is worthless. A serious SEO partner tracks qualified leads or revenue attributable to organic search — and can show you that trend over at least six months for an existing client.

Technical audit first, content second

Broken indexing, slow load times, missing structured data and crawl errors will cap your rankings no matter how much content you publish. Any agency that jumps straight to a content calendar without a technical audit is skipping the foundation.

Local SEO is a different skill — check for it specifically

If you have a physical location or serve a specific city or region, your Google Business Profile, NAP consistency and local landing pages matter as much as blog content. Ask directly how the agency handles local SEO — many "SEO agencies" only do content and skip this entirely.

AI search visibility is now part of the job

A growing share of research happens inside ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews before a searcher ever clicks a blue link. Ask how the agency structures content and schema markup to be citable by AI systems — an agency with no answer here is optimising for 2020, not 2026.

Get specific about link building

Vague answers like "we have relationships" or "our partner network" are a red flag. A trustworthy agency will explain exactly how links are earned or built — guest content, digital PR, resource link building — and will never promise a specific number of links per month, which is a hallmark of low-quality link farms.

Page-one rankings for the wrong keyword are worth exactly nothing. Ask for organic revenue instead.

Frequently asked

How long does SEO take to show results in India?

Meaningful movement typically starts in 3-4 months for a technically healthy site, with compounding gains over 6-12 months. Anyone promising page-one rankings in weeks is either targeting near-zero-competition keywords or cutting corners.

How much does SEO cost in Delhi NCR?

A comprehensive SEO retainer — technical, content and local — typically starts around ₹89,999/month for a competitive category; smaller scopes can be lower but usually skip one of the three pillars.

Is SEO still worth it with AI search growing?

Yes — AI answer engines still pull from indexed, well-structured web content, so strong SEO fundamentals are what make a brand citable inside AI Overviews and chatbot answers, not a separate discipline.
