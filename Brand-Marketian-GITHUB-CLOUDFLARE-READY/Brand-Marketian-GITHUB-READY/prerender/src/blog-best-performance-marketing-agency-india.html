How to choose the best performance marketing agency in India

Every agency in India will show you a screenshot of a good month. Here is what to check before you hand over your ad budget.

BM Brand Marketian ·Sep 2026 ·7 min read

Key takeaways

  • ROAS and cost per lead should be reported weekly, by channel — not bundled into one "performance" number.
  • Your ad accounts (Meta, Google, YouTube) should sit in your name from day one, not the agency's.
  • Ask to see a live account, not just a case study slide — screenshots can be dated.
  • Creative testing volume matters more than media-buying cleverness once accounts mature.
  • A good agency tells you when to stop spending, not just when to spend more.

Search "best performance marketing agency in India" and every result claims to 10x your ROAS. Performance marketing is measurable by definition, so there is no excuse for an agency that cannot show you real numbers before you sign. Here is what actually separates the agencies worth paying from the ones renting your budget.

Start with the account, not the pitch deck

Any agency can build a slide with a client's best month highlighted. Ask instead to see a live ad account — even a read-only view — with real spend, real cost per lead and real return over at least 90 days. If an agency resists this, treat it as your answer.

Weekly numbers, by channel, not a monthly PDF

Meta, Google and YouTube behave differently and need different optimisation cadences. A serious performance partner reports cost per lead and ROAS separately for each channel, every week — not a single blended "performance" number buried in a monthly PDF three weeks after the fact.

Who owns the account matters more than who runs it

Ad accounts, pixels, conversion APIs and analytics should be created in your business name and shared with the agency as a partner — never the reverse. This is the single biggest protection against losing your data and history if you ever switch agencies.

Creative volume beats media-buying tricks

Once an account account is technically healthy, the biggest lever left is creative testing — new hooks, formats and angles shipped weekly. An agency that talks only about bidding strategy and audience stacking, and never about a creative testing calendar, is optimising the smaller lever.

A good agency tells you to spend less, sometimes

Watch for an agency that only ever recommends increasing budget. The honest ones will tell you when creative fatigue has set in, when a category is seasonally weak, or when the funnel below the ad — landing page, follow-up — is the real bottleneck, not the media spend.

If an agency will not show you a live account before you sign, assume the screenshots are the best month they have ever had.

Frequently asked

How much does performance marketing cost in India?

Retainers for a managed performance marketing service in India typically start around ₹49,999/month, separate from ad spend, scaling with account complexity and spend volume.

What is a good ROAS for Indian brands?

It varies heavily by category and margin — a D2C brand with 40% margins might target 3x+ ROAS, while a low-margin category may need 6x or higher just to break even. Ask any agency what ROAS they consider healthy for your specific category before comparing numbers.

Should I run performance marketing without SEO?

You can, but performance marketing alone means every rupee of traffic stops the day you stop paying. Pairing it with SEO and content builds compounding organic traffic that keeps converting long after a campaign ends.
