How to choose the best website design company in Delhi NCR

A good-looking website that does not convert is a waste of budget. Here is how to pick a web design company that builds sites that sell.

BM Brand Marketian ·Sep 2026 ·7 min read

Key takeaways

  • Portfolio screenshots do not show speed or mobile performance — test the live sites yourself.
  • SEO-ready structure should be built in from day one, not bolted on after launch.
  • Mobile-first is not optional in India — most traffic for most categories is mobile.
  • Ask what happens after launch: hosting, updates, and who owns the domain and code.
  • A conversion-focused build (clear CTAs, fast forms, WhatsApp integration) matters more than visual trends.

Delhi NCR has a large web design and development market, and portfolios can be deceiving — a beautiful screenshot says nothing about load time, mobile usability or whether the site actually converts visitors into enquiries. Here is what to check before commissioning a new website.

Test the live sites, not the screenshots

Open three or four sites from the agency's portfolio on your own phone. Check load time, whether forms work smoothly, and how the site feels to navigate on mobile. A portfolio full of beautiful desktop screenshots that load slowly on mobile is a warning sign.

SEO-ready structure is a build decision, not an add-on

Clean URL structure, proper heading hierarchy, fast load times and structured data all need to be decided during the build, not patched in afterward. Ask specifically whether the agency builds with SEO fundamentals in mind from the start.

Mobile-first, because most of India is mobile-first

For most consumer categories in India, the majority of traffic is mobile. A site designed for desktop first and adapted for mobile as an afterthought usually shows — slow load times, awkward tap targets, forms that are painful to fill on a phone.

Ask about after launch, not just before

Who hosts the site, who owns the domain, and who handles updates after launch? Confirm this upfront — some agencies build the site but retain hosting or domain control in ways that make switching providers later difficult.

Conversion features beat visual trends

Clear calls to action, fast-loading contact forms, and WhatsApp click-to-chat integration usually move the needle on enquiries more than the latest animation trend. Ask how the agency thinks about conversion, not just aesthetics.

A beautiful website that loads slowly on a phone in Delhi traffic is not a beautiful website — it is a bounced visitor.

Frequently asked

How much does a website cost in Delhi NCR?

Pricing is typically custom-quoted based on scope — a simple brochure site costs far less than a custom e-commerce build with CMS and integrations, so get a detailed quote against your specific requirements.

How long does a website build take?

A standard business website usually takes 4-8 weeks from kickoff to launch, longer for e-commerce or custom-feature builds, depending on how quickly content and approvals move.

Should I use WordPress or a custom build?

It depends on your needs — WordPress suits content-heavy sites needing frequent updates by non-technical staff, while a custom build often performs better for speed and specific functionality. Ask any agency to justify their recommendation against your actual use case.
