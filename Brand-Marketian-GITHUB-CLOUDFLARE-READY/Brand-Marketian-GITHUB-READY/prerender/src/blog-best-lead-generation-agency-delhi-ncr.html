How to choose the best lead generation agency in Delhi NCR

Raw form fills are not leads. Here is how to pick a lead gen agency whose leads your sales team actually wants to call.

BM Brand Marketian ·Sep 2026 ·7 min read

Key takeaways

  • Define "qualified lead" together with the agency in week one — raw form fills inflate numbers without meaning.
  • WhatsApp follow-up converts far better than email alone for most Indian audiences.
  • Ask how fast a new lead gets a first follow-up — speed to contact is one of the biggest conversion levers.
  • The offer and landing page matter as much as the ad driving traffic to them.
  • A good lead gen partner reports cost per qualified lead, and ideally cost per sale, not just lead volume.

Delhi NCR agencies selling "lead generation" often measure success in raw form-fill volume — a number that is easy to inflate and hard to sell against. A sales team drowning in unqualified leads is not a win. Here is what to check before hiring a lead generation partner.

Define "qualified lead" before starting, not after

Every category has a different bar for what counts as a real, sales-ready lead. Agree this definition with the agency in week one, and hold them to reporting against it — not raw form submissions, which can be inflated with low-intent traffic.

WhatsApp follow-up beats email for most Indian audiences

Across most consumer and even B2B categories in India, WhatsApp reply rates consistently beat email. Ask whether the agency's lead flow routes through WhatsApp automatically, or whether every lead still gets funnelled through an email inbox nobody checks quickly.

Speed to first contact is a bigger lever than most people think

A lead contacted within five minutes converts dramatically better than one contacted an hour later. Ask how fast the follow-up system responds to a new lead — this single factor often explains why two agencies with similar traffic get very different close rates.

The landing page and offer carry half the weight

A great ad driving traffic to a slow, confusing landing page with a weak offer will underperform a mediocre ad sending traffic to a fast, clear page. Ask whether the agency owns landing page and offer strategy, or only runs the ad and leaves the rest to you.

Report cost per qualified lead, not just volume

A high volume of cheap, unqualified leads looks good on a dashboard and wastes your sales team's time. Insist on cost per qualified lead as the headline metric, and where possible, cost per sale — volume alone is a vanity number.

A hundred unqualified leads a month is not a lead generation win. It is a hundred wasted sales calls.

Frequently asked

What is a good cost per lead in Delhi NCR?

It varies enormously by category and ticket size — a high-value B2B service can sustain a much higher cost per lead than a low-ticket consumer product. Ask any agency for category-specific benchmarks rather than a single generic number.

How fast should a new lead be contacted?

Ideally within five minutes — response rates and conversion drop sharply after the first 30 minutes, and even faster in competitive categories where the buyer is likely contacting multiple options at once.

Is WhatsApp legal and reliable for lead follow-up in India?

Yes, when done through proper opt-in and the WhatsApp Business API — a compliant setup with clear consent is standard practice and significantly outperforms email-only follow-up for response rates.
