How to choose the best content marketing agency in India

Most "content marketing" in India is a blog nobody reads. Here is how to find an agency that ties content to actual leads.

BM Brand Marketian ·Sep 2026 ·7 min read

Key takeaways

  • Publishing volume (posts per month) is the wrong metric — ask about traffic and leads per published piece instead.
  • Content should be planned around the buyer journey, not a random keyword list.
  • A human editor should own every piece, even when AI assists research and drafting.
  • Ask how content connects to SEO, paid ads and email/WhatsApp — content in a silo underperforms.
  • Request to see actual published work, not just a services page description.

Content marketing in India often means a blog that gets updated twice a month with generic 500-word posts nobody reads or ranks for. That is publishing, not content marketing. Here is how to tell the difference before you sign a retainer.

Volume is not the metric — traffic and leads per piece are

An agency proud of "8 posts a month" is optimising for the wrong thing. Ask instead how much organic traffic and how many leads their published content generates per piece, on average, for an existing client — even directionally.

Content should map to the buyer journey

Good content marketing plans pieces around where a buyer actually is — early awareness questions, comparison content when they are evaluating options, and bottom-of-funnel content like case studies when they are close to deciding. A random keyword list with no funnel logic behind it produces disconnected content.

AI-assisted is fine. AI-only is not

Most serious agencies now use AI to speed up research and first drafts — that is normal in 2026. What matters is whether a human writer and editor own every published piece for accuracy, voice and genuine usefulness, rather than publishing raw AI output at scale.

Content that lives in a silo underperforms

The strongest content programs feed SEO (ranking), paid ads (as landing pages or ad angles) and email/WhatsApp nurture sequences from the same core pieces. Ask whether content strategy is planned alongside SEO and performance marketing, or handed off separately with no coordination.

Ask to see real published work

A services page description tells you nothing. Ask for three to five live examples of published content, ideally in a similar industry, and judge for yourself whether it reads like something a real person would finish.

Eight posts a month that nobody reads is not a content strategy. It is a publishing schedule.

Frequently asked

Is content marketing worth it for a new brand?

It compounds slower than paid ads but keeps paying after you stop investing, unlike ad spend. Most brands benefit from running both together — ads for immediate demand, content for long-term organic traffic.

How is content marketing priced in India?

Most agencies scope it per plan based on volume and complexity rather than a flat monthly fee, since a landing page, a blog post and a case study require very different effort.

Is AI-written content bad for SEO?

Not inherently — what matters to search engines and readers is whether the content is genuinely useful and accurate. AI-assisted drafts edited and fact-checked by a human perform fine; raw unedited AI output at scale usually does not.
